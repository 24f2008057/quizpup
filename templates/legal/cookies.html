The short version

QuizPup uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work. It uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies at the moment. If we add optional ones later, they will stay off until you allow them.

What we store

Cookies and browser storage used by QuizPup
Name Purpose Lasts Type
session (cookie) Keeps you signed in and holds the security token that protects forms Up to {{ config['PERMANENT_SESSION_LIFETIME'].days }} days Strictly necessary
remember_token (cookie) Keeps you signed in after you close the browser Up to {{ config['REMEMBER_COOKIE_DURATION'].days }} days Strictly necessary
quizpup_consent (local storage) Remembers your cookie choice Until you clear it Strictly necessary
isLoggedIn (local storage) Lets the app tell whether you are signed in Until you log out or clear it Strictly necessary
Supabase sign-in session (local storage, on sign-in pages) Completes Google and GitHub sign-in Until you clear it Strictly necessary
quizpup_splash_shown (session storage) Shows the opening screen once per visit Until you close the tab Strictly necessary

Payment window

When you choose to pay, QuizPup opens a payment window from Razorpay. Razorpay may set its own cookies or storage for fraud prevention and to run the payment. We do not load it until you click Continue to payment. See Razorpay's own policies for details.

Managing cookies

You can change your choice at any time with the button above or from the account menu. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but QuizPup will not keep you signed in without the cookies listed above.
