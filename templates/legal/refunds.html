How refunds will work

  • Refund window for a first purchase: 3 days from the payment.
  • Renewals: a renewal can be refunded within 3 days of the renewal payment.
  • Cancelling, in QuizPup or by stopping the autopay with your bank, stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method, including UPI, within 7 business days of us approving the refund.
  • If you were charged by mistake or twice, we will refund it in full.
  • How to ask: use the {{ link('privacy.contact', 'Contact page') }} and include the email on your account.
