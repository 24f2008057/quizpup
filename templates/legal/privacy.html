Who we are

QuizPup is run by {{ ph(legal.entity, 'legal name of the operator; no business is registered yet') }}, {{ ph(legal.address, 'registered or postal address') }}. We decide why and how your personal data is used. You can reach us on the {{ link('privacy.contact', 'Contact page') }}.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, first and last name, email address and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name and verified email from them instead of a password.
  • Your age group (adult or under 18). If you are under 18, we also keep your parent or guardian's email address and a record of their approval.
  • Your study content: topics you enter, quizzes and the answers you give, scores, flashcard decks, and notes (text extracted from files you upload, the summary and the PDF we make).
  • Files you upload (images and PDFs). They are kept only while they are being processed and are deleted afterwards.
  • Records of what you agreed to: which Terms and Privacy Policy version, when, and how.
  • Short-lived codes for email verification, password reset and email change, and the time you created your account and last signed in.
  • Technical logs kept by our host: your user id, the type of event and error messages.

We do not collect your precise location, device or advertising identifiers, or payment details. We no longer ask for a phone number. {{ todo('if existing phone numbers are still stored, say so here or delete them first') }}

Why we use it, and our basis

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure.
  • To generate and store the quizzes, flashcards and notes you ask for.
  • To send you emails you need, such as verification and password-reset codes.
  • To keep proof of what you agreed to, and to follow the law.

Topics you type and files you upload are sent to Google's Gemini API to create your quizzes, flashcards and notes. We do not send your name, email or user id with them.
