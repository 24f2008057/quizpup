What we collect

Your name, username, email address and a hashed password when you sign up. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name and verified email from that provider. We also store the quizzes, flashcards and notes you create, your answers and scores, and a record of when you accepted our policies. We do not ask for your phone number.

How AI is used

Topics you enter and files you upload are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate quizzes, flashcards and notes. We do not send your name or email to Gemini. QuizPup does not train AI models on your content. {% if ai_free_tier %}On our current Google plan, Google may use submitted content to improve its products and people may review it, so please do not upload personal information.{% endif %}

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep data

Until you delete it or your account. Unverified sign-ups are removed after a few days, and accounts with no sign-in for about two years are removed.

Your rights

You can correct your details, download your data and delete your account from your Profile page at any time.
