# Data Processing Agreements and Sub-processors (items 27 and 29)

Status: not complete. A data processing agreement is a contract that has to be accepted or signed with each provider. I cannot do that for you, and QuizPup has no registered business yet, so decide whose name signs.

Public list (item 29): `/subprocessors` in the app. Fill the location cells after checking each dashboard.

| Provider | Role | Agreement to obtain | Where to look | Done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, auth | Data Processing Addendum | Supabase legal or trust pages, or the organisation settings | No |
| Render | Hosting | Data Processing Addendum | Render legal pages or account settings | No |
| Google (Gemini API) | AI generation | Data Processing terms for the paid Gemini API; free tier terms differ | Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and Google's data-processing terms for the paid service | No |
| Brevo | Email | Data Processing Agreement | Brevo legal pages or account settings | No |
| Razorpay | Payments (Workstream 7) | Merchant agreement | Razorpay dashboard | Not yet in use |

For each, record: date accepted, who accepted, hosting region, and backup retention. Also check whether each provider's terms require a registered business.

Before launch ask a lawyer whether a cross-border transfer notice is needed, since several providers may process data outside India.
